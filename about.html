Compliance and risk

Shani Olade, CCEP, CHC

Executive Advisor

Shani builds compliance programs that hold up under a regulator's review and still make sense to the people who run them. For more than a decade she has designed and matured compliance, privacy and risk programs, often from the ground up, across Fortune 50 health plans, fast-growing MSOs and health technology platforms. As a VP of Compliance and Privacy and chief compliance officer, she owns CPOM, Anti-Kickback and Stark risk, HIPAA privacy and investigations, and AI governance for a multi-state telehealth organization. She pairs that regulatory depth with practical systems: SOC 2 and HITRUST readiness, risk-based audit plans, and executive dashboards that show whether a program is actually working.

Why she leads alignment checks

  • She has run the audit you are preparing for. At a Fortune 50 health plan she was the main contact for state and federal regulators, and her teams passed CMS, state and federal audits again and again, helping the company avoid multi-million-dollar penalties.
  • She builds programs from the ground up. She built a new Medicare Advantage plan's compliance program and secured the CMS approval it needed to launch membership. Today she is building the full compliance, privacy and risk program for a multi-state telehealth MSO.
  • She checks the handoffs, not just the policies. As a director of Enterprise Delegation Oversight, she led teams of up to 30 and oversaw compliance across 26+ products and 160+ provider groups, including credentialing, delegation agreements and delegate privacy incidents.
  • She fixes problems before regulators find them. She turned corrective action planning into a data-driven early warning process, cutting necessary corrective actions by 8%. At a large medical group she raised audit scores by an average of 12% in three months.
  • She knows the risks that come with growth. She manages CPOM, Anti-Kickback, Stark and fee-splitting risk as an MSO expands state by state, and she has run fraud, waste and abuse investigations and coding and clinical integrity reviews.
  • She keeps pace with technology and privacy. At a healthcare technology company she led SOC 2 and HITRUST readiness and advised on HIPAA, GDPR and CCPA. She has also designed AI governance frameworks to review new tools before teams adopt them.

Clinical and operations

Laura Geiger, DNP, FNP-BC, NEA-BC

Executive Advisor

Laura started as an emergency nurse practitioner and rose to chief clinical officer. She has overseen clinical workflows, quality initiatives and complex care programs across Medicare, Medicaid and commercial populations. She knows what a policy looks like at the bedside, and what it costs when it does not work there.

Why she leads alignment checks

  • She has worked the front line. She spent nine years as an emergency and lead nurse practitioner at TeamHealth, UPMC and Envision Healthcare before moving into operations.
  • She has built care models from the ground up. In eight years at Optum she went from team lead of the Florida HouseCalls program to regional director of corporate clinical operations for the Complex Care Model.
  • She has led national programs. At Elevance Health she was National Director of ISNP and DCE programs at CareMore Health, and Clinical Director of Operations at Aspire Health.
  • She has run clinical operations at the executive level. She has been Director of Nurse Practitioners at Devoted Health, Regional Vice President of Clinical Operations at Longevity Health Plan and a chief clinical officer.
  • She connects quality to compliance. Her work joins evidence-based practice, regulatory compliance and resource utilization, so clinical measures do not stay in a binder.
