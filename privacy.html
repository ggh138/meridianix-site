Privacy policy

The short version

We collect only what you send us, and we use it only to reply.

  • Our contact form collects your name, work email, company and message.
  • We use it to answer your request and set up an alignment check. Nothing else.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising cookies or tracking pixels.
  • We do not sell or rent your information, and we never will.

Who we are

Meridianix Advisory ("Meridianix," "we," "us") provides operations, compliance, clinical and HR advisory services. This policy covers information collected through our website, meridianixadvisory.com.

It does not cover client engagements. When we work with a client, our signed engagement agreement governs how we handle that client's information. Where an engagement involves protected health information, we handle it under a business associate agreement as HIPAA requires.

What we collect

Information you give us

When you use the contact form to request an alignment check, we collect:

  • Your full name and work email address
  • Your company name, if you provide it
  • The area you selected (operations, compliance, clinical, HR or not sure yet)
  • Anything you write in the message field

Please do not send patient information or confidential business information through the form. We will ask for what we need once we have agreed how to protect it.

Information collected automatically

Like nearly every website, the server that hosts our site keeps standard technical logs when you visit. These include your IP address, browser type, the pages you request, the referring page and the date and time. They are used to keep the site running and secure.

What we do not collect

Our website does not use analytics tools, advertising or tracking cookies, social media pixels or session recording. The site itself sets no cookies.

How we use it

We use the information you send us to:

  • Reply to your request and schedule an alignment check
  • Follow up about that request and any work that comes from it
  • Keep our website secure and working properly
  • Meet our legal obligations

We will not add you to a marketing list unless you ask us to. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that make our website work, and only as much as they need:

  • Formspree receives contact form submissions and forwards them to us by email. Formspree stores submissions under its own privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typeface on this site. When your browser loads it, Google receives your IP address. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Our web hosting and email providers store and deliver the site and our messages.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a merger or sale of our business. In that case this policy would continue to apply to your information.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries for as long as we need them to respond and to manage any relationship that follows. After that we delete them, unless the law requires us to keep them longer. Server logs are kept by our hosting provider for a limited period and then deleted.

How we protect it

Form submissions travel over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. We limit access to inquiries to the people at Meridianix who need them to respond. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what you send us.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you
  • Correct information that is wrong
  • Delete your information
  • Stop contacting you

Depending on where you live, state or international privacy laws may give you additional rights. We honor requests regardless of where you live, and we will not treat you differently for making one. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

Because we do not track visitors, there is nothing for a browser's "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal to turn off.

Children

Our website is meant for businesses and professionals. It is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will say so clearly on this page.

Contact us

To make a request or ask a question about this policy, use the contact form and start your message with "Privacy request." We will respond within 30 days.
